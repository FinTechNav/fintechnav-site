All research deliverables, reports, backtest logs, charts, and strategy artifacts must be placed in:
`/Users/bradjensen/fintechnav-site/artifacts/`

2. Keep a local research catalog under `artifacts/local/`. Reserve the committed `artifacts/INDEX.md` for reviewed, non-sensitive engineering records.
