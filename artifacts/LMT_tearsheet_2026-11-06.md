## Financial results and business case

Third-quarter sales were $20.7 billion, up 11%; EPS was $7.97. Backlog reached $292 billion. Management's 2026 outlook calls for $80–82 billion sales, $30.35–30.75 EPS and $7.8–8.0 billion free cash flow. These are forecasts, and backlog is not immediate revenue or guaranteed profit. [Company results](https://news.lockheedmartin.com/2026-10-22-Lockheed-Martin-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results).

The positive thesis depends on converting orders into profitable deliveries and cash. Risks include funding changes, F-35 execution, supplier constraints and losses on fixed-price contracts. The dividend remains subject to board decisions. [Company risk disclosures](https://news.lockheedmartin.com/2026-10-22-Lockheed-Martin-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results).
