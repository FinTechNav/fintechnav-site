### Detailed Breakdown & Tradeoffs

1. **`CIFR` — Cipher Digital Inc. (~$7.27–$7.29/share, ~$2.8B market cap)**
   - **Upside Driver:** Holds $16.6 billion in contracted HPC leases across 700 MW, anchored by Black Pearl and a 20-year, $9.1 billion lease at Barber Lake. Recorded its first $5.9 million in HPC lease revenue in Q3 2026. Development pipeline includes the 1 GW Colchis project and up to 2.5 GW of on-site natural gas generation potential.
   - **Risk / Tradeoff:** High capital expenditure requirements to build out data halls; potential equity or convertible-note dilution during construction.

2. **`CLSK` — CleanSpark, Inc. (~$7.77/share, ~$1.96B–$2.1B market cap)**
   - **Upside Driver:** Executed a 20-year triple-net HPC infrastructure lease at its Sandersville, Georgia campus (175 MW critical IT load) with an investment-grade technology tenant for $6.6 billion in base contracted revenue (up to $11.6 billion with extension options). Build-out is funded via a $2.276 billion senior secured notes offering.
   - **Risk / Tradeoff:** Initial power delivery does not begin until Q4 2027, whereas `CIFR` is already collecting HPC rent and holds 2.5x more base contracted backlog.

3. **`HIVE` — HIVE Digital Technologies Ltd. (~$1.91–$1.94/share, ~$500M–$535M market cap)**
   - **Upside Driver:** Operates the `BUZZ HPC` subsidiary, which signed a 5-year, $350 million total contract value (~$70 million ARR) agreement deploying NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra (`GB300 NVL72`) clusters in Merritt, British Columbia. Targets ~$180 million in annualized contracted HPC revenue with a >$1 billion pipeline.
   - **Risk / Tradeoff:** Five-year GPU cloud contracts carry faster hardware depreciation cycles than 20-year triple-net land and power leases.

4. **`EOSE` — Eos Energy Enterprises, Inc. (~$1.84/share, ~$690M–$820M market cap)**
   - **Upside Driver:** Supplies zinc-based long-duration energy storage (LDES) systems for grid and data center backup power, backed by a ~$765 million (3.2 GWh) commercial backlog.
   - **Risk / Tradeoff:** Hardware/equipment manufacturing margin and scaling risk rather than recurring 20-year landlord lease cash flows.

5. **`POET` — POET Technologies Inc. (~$5.15/share, ~$868M–$900M market cap)**
   - **Upside Driver:** Designs optical interposers and photonic integrated circuits that solve high-speed data interconnect bottlenecks between AI GPU clusters inside data centers.
   - **Risk / Tradeoff:** Early-stage component supplier without multi-billion-dollar contracted revenue backlogs.
